## Overview

I do a lot of illustration for Omniplex as the brand moves forward into global status. This is the repository for the new stuff.

## Approach

It's been a bunch of fun carrying these through into more and more projects because there's a small narrative starting to build up.

## Illustration Types

- Product feature illustrations
- Marketing/landing page graphics
- Email campaign visuals
- Brand identity elements
- Animated GIFs for case studies

## Style

Consistent illustrative style that evolved with the Omniplex brand as the company expanded globally.
