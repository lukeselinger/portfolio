## Overview

The original blog was built on Wordpress and by the time I had joined Omniplex, they had been moving into using the HubSpot COS.

## Goals

1. Redesign the blog to fully take advantage of the features the COS offered
2. Decrease bounce & exit rates
3. Improve responsive design (critical for search ranking)

## Platform Migration

Migrated from WordPress to HubSpot COS (Content Optimization System).

## Key Outcomes

- Improved responsive design
- Better SEO performance
- Enhanced content management capabilities through HubSpot
