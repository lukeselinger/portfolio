## Overview

Occipital approached the AUX team to perform some usability testing on their 3D camera, the Structure Sensor. They had built the hardware and a handful of demo apps and wanted developers to download their SDK and build on top of what they had.

## Goal

The goal of this project was to figure out how people were using the demo apps and identify any opportunities within the user experience.

## Process

Structure Sensor Physical Prototype Test conducted with the AUX team.

## Outcomes

*Documentation of usability findings and recommendations for improving the Structure Sensor experience.*
